## Full Description

**Guard your time. Honor your prayers.**

Noor Guard is a warm, all-in-one companion for Muslims everywhere — and especially for new Muslims and reverts taking their first steps in salah. Available in English, Arabic, Urdu, and Mexican Spanish, Noor Guard meets you in the language that feels like home.

**🕌 Prayer Times & Reminders**
Accurate daily prayer times for your exact location, gentle notifications before each salah, and simple sunnah guidance so you always know what to recite and how to pray with confidence.

**🔥 Prayer Streak Tracker**
Mark each prayer as you complete it, build your streak, and stay motivated to never miss a salah.

**📖 Full Quran Reader & Tafsir**
Read the complete Quran in Arabic with translations, plus simplified Tafsir from Ibn Kathir to help you understand the meaning and message behind every verse.

**🤲 53 Duas in 4 Languages**
A growing library of authentic duas for everyday moments — morning and evening, before meals, while traveling, and more — each with Arabic text, transliteration, and translation.

**🧭 Qibla Compass**
Find the direction of the Kaaba instantly, anywhere in the world.

**🗓️ Islamic Calendar**
Track Hijri dates and important Islamic occasions throughout the year.

**🎯 Focus Mode**
Set distraction-free sessions for Quran study, reflection, or simply disconnecting from the noise.

**🌙 Revert Corner**
A dedicated home for new Muslims, with a Wudu Guide, a step-by-step How to Pray walkthrough, an Islamic Glossary, a friendly "Why Do We...?" explainer, real Community Stories from fellow reverts, and a 30-Day New Muslim Checklist to guide your first month.

**🧡 Beginner Mode**
Extra explanations and gentle tips throughout the app for anyone just starting their journey — no jargon, no judgment.

**🔐 Privacy First. Always.**
No ads. No tracking. No accounts required. Your prayers, streaks, and progress stay on your device, under your control — always.

Whether you said your shahada yesterday or have been practicing for decades, Noor Guard meets you exactly where you are.

**Guard your time. Honor your prayers.**
