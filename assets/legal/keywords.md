# App Store & Google Play Keywords — Noor Guard

## Apple App Store — Keyword Field (≤100 characters total)

Apple's keyword field is a single comma-separated string with a strict 100-character limit. No need to repeat words already in the app name/subtitle ("Noor Guard," "Islamic," "Prayer," "Focus" are already covered there, but a few are repeated below for indexing strength since Apple weighs the keyword field heavily).

**Recommended string (91 characters):**

```
prayer times,islam,muslim,quran,qibla,salah,duas,revert,new muslim,halal,azan,salat,oracion
```

**Ranked priority (highest estimated search volume first):**

1. `prayer times` — core, highest-intent search term
2. `islam` — broad category anchor
3. `muslim` — broad category anchor
4. `quran` — major feature, high search volume
5. `qibla` — major feature, dedicated searches
6. `salah` — Arabic/English term for prayer, frequently searched
7. `duas` — feature-specific, growing search interest
8. `revert` — niche but high-intent (matches our positioning)
9. `new muslim` — niche but high-intent (matches our positioning)
10. `halal` — broad lifestyle term, strong cross-search traffic
11. `azan` — Arabic term for the call to prayer (transliteration variant)
12. `salat` — alternate transliteration of "salah" (captures spelling variants)
13. `oracion` — Spanish for "prayer" (captures Spanish-language searches)

> Tip: Apple's search algorithm also indexes the app name and subtitle, so avoid duplicating "Noor Guard," "Islamic," "Prayer," or "Focus" here — that space is better spent on additional unique terms like the ones above.

## Google Play — Short & Long Description Keyword Targets

Google Play doesn't have a dedicated keyword field — instead, ranking comes from natural keyword placement in the app's title, short description, and full description. Use the terms below organically and repeat the highest-priority ones 2–3 times across the listing.

**Primary keywords (use in title/short description):**
- prayer times
- Islamic app
- Muslim app
- Qibla compass
- Quran reader

**Secondary keywords (weave into the full description):**
- salah / salat
- duas / dua list
- new Muslim
- revert / convert to Islam
- prayer reminder
- prayer streak / prayer tracker
- focus mode / app blocker
- Islamic lock screen
- halal lifestyle
- azan / adhan times

**Long-tail keywords (great for description body & ASO experiments):**
- "prayer times app for Muslims"
- "Qibla finder and compass"
- "Quran app with translation"
- "duas for daily life"
- "app for new Muslims and reverts"
- "Islamic focus and productivity app"
- "prayer streak tracker app"
