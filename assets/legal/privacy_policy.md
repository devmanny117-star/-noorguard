# Privacy Policy — Noor Guard: Islamic Prayer & Focus

**Last updated:** June 7, 2026

As-salamu alaykum, and welcome.

Noor Guard was built to help you stay connected to your prayers and your deen — not to collect your data. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information the app uses, why it uses it, and (just as importantly) what it never touches. By using Noor Guard, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

Noor Guard is developed and maintained by an independent developer, not a large company. We take your trust seriously, and we've designed the app from the ground up to need as little of your information as possible.

## 1. The Short Version

- We don't ask you to create an account.
- We don't collect your name, email, phone number, or any personal identifiers.
- We don't sell your data — to anyone, ever.

## 2. Information Noor Guard Uses

### a) Device Location
Noor Guard uses your device's location **solely** to:
- Calculate accurate prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) for your area, and
- Determine the direction of the Qibla for the compass feature.

Your location is processed on your device. To calculate prayer times, your approximate coordinates are sent to the Aladhan API (see Section 4 for details on this and other third-party services); the Qibla direction, by contrast, is calculated entirely on your device using your phone's compass and GPS and is never transmitted anywhere. In either case, your location is not stored on our servers (we don't have any), not linked to your identity, and not used for advertising or analytics of any kind. You can deny or revoke location permissions at any time in your device settings — Noor Guard will simply ask you to enter a city manually instead.

### b) Notification Preferences
If you choose to enable prayer reminders or focus-session alerts, Noor Guard stores your notification settings (such as which prayers you'd like to be reminded about, and how far in advance) directly on your device using your operating system's notification framework. This information never leaves your phone.

### c) Prayer Completion & Streak Data
When you mark a prayer as completed, start a focus session, or build a streak, that information is saved **locally on your device only**. We have no servers that receive, store, or back up this data. If you delete the app, this information is gone — it was never anywhere else.

## 5. Third-Party Services We Rely On

To provide certain features, Noor Guard connects to a small number of trusted, purpose-built services. These services receive only the minimal information required to perform their specific function (for example, your approximate coordinates, sent solely to calculate prayer times) — never your identity.

- **Aladhan API** — used to calculate accurate daily prayer times based on your location or chosen city. [aladhan.com](https://aladhan.com)
- **AlQuran Cloud API** — used to fetch Quranic text and translations for the in-app Quran reader. [alquran.cloud](https://alquran.cloud)
- **Google Fonts** — used to load the typefaces (including Arabic script fonts) that give Noor Guard its look and feel. [fonts.google.com](https://fonts.google.com)

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy and terms, which govern any data exchanged directly with them when the app makes a request on your behalf. We encourage you to review their policies if you'd like further detail.

Because these services may operate servers located outside your home country, any data sent to them (such as approximate coordinates used to calculate prayer times) may be transmitted across borders. This transmission is momentary, used only to return the requested information to your device, is not stored by us, and is governed by each provider's own privacy practices.

## 6. Data Storage & Security

All personal app data — your streaks, completed prayers, focus history, language preference, and settings — is stored locally on your device using standard, secure on-device storage. Nothing is uploaded to a cloud server owned or operated by us, because no such server exists.

Because your data stays on your device, you remain in full control of it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data through your device settings, permanently removes this locally stored information.

While we've designed Noor Guard to avoid handling sensitive data in the first place, please note that no method of electronic storage or transmission is ever 100% secure. We rely on the security protections built into your device's operating system (iOS and Android) to help safeguard locally stored app data, and we encourage you to keep your device's operating system up to date.

## 7. Your Privacy Choices & Rights

Even though Noor Guard does not collect personal data on remote servers, you remain in control of the limited information the app does use:

- **Location permissions** — Grant, deny, or revoke location access at any time through your device's Settings app. If you deny access, you can still use Noor Guard by entering your city manually.
- **Notification permissions** — Turn prayer and focus reminders on or off, or disable notifications entirely, through your device's notification settings.
- **Local data deletion** — Because your streaks, history, and preferences are stored only on your device, you can erase them at any time by clearing the app's storage/data or uninstalling Noor Guard.
- **Regional privacy rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws)** — If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California, or another region with data protection laws, you may have rights such as the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal data, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because Noor Guard does not collect or store personal data on any server we control, there is generally no remote personal data for us to provide, correct, or erase — it lives on your device, under your control. If you ever believe we hold personal information about you, please contact us using the details in Section 12 and we will look into it promptly and at no cost to you.
