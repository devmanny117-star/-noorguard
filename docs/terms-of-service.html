2. Description of Service

Noor Guard is an Islamic prayer and productivity companion available for iOS and Android. Core features — prayer times, Qibla direction, the Quran reader, duas, the Islamic calendar, and the 99 Names of Allah — are available on both platforms.

Two features are currently Android only: App Blocking (which restricts distracting apps during prayer and focus time) and the Islamic Lock Screen (which replaces your device's lock screen with prayer times and reminders). These are described in detail in Sections 3 and 4 below. We may expand these features to iOS in the future; this document will be updated if and when that happens.

3. App Blocking and Accessibility Service (Android Only)

App Blocking is an optional feature that helps you avoid distracting apps during prayer times and focus sessions. To function, it requires you to explicitly grant Android's Accessibility Service permission through your device settings — Noor Guard will never enable this permission on your behalf.

This permission is used solely to detect when the foreground app on your device changes, so Noor Guard knows which app is currently open and can block it if it's on your blocked list during an active prayer or focus window. Noor Guard's Accessibility Service usage does not:

  • Read the content of your screen,
  • Collect keystrokes or anything you type, or
  • Transmit any data off your device.

You can revoke Accessibility Service access at any time through your device's Settings app, which immediately disables App Blocking. Doing so does not affect any other feature of Noor Guard.

4. Islamic Lock Screen (Android Only)

The Islamic Lock Screen is an optional feature that overlays prayer times, Quranic verses, and reminders on top of your device's normal lock screen. To function, it requires the "Draw Over Other Apps" permission through your device settings.

Enabling this feature does not compromise your device's security in any way: your device's PIN, pattern, or biometric lock (fingerprint/face unlock) remains fully active and is still required to unlock your device. The Islamic Lock Screen is a visual overlay only — it never replaces or bypasses your device's actual lock mechanism.

You can disable the Islamic Lock Screen at any time in Noor Guard's Settings, which immediately restores your device's normal lock screen appearance.
